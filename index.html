WHAT HAPPENS TO YOUR MAIL

What is read
Only the email you forward. I have no connection to your inbox — I could not read anything else if I tried.
How it is processed
By a fixed set of rules running inside the intake mailbox on Google's servers. No AI model, no third-party service ever sees the text. The rules are deterministic: the same email always produces the same three lines.
How long it is kept
Seven days, then permanently deleted — trash emptied by the same job. Nothing is stored anywhere else. Your own copy, of course, stays in your inbox.
Who can see it
No one reads it. One person runs this — Adil Mannan, Toronto — and the mailbox is 2-step-verified. Usage is counted with a one-way salted hash, so I can see how many people use Measure, never who.
What is sent
Nothing, to anyone, on your behalf. The summary returns only to the authenticated address that forwarded it. Attachments are carried back to you unopened — never scanned, never read.
How to stop
Stop forwarding. To have anything deleted sooner, email adil.mannan1@gmail.com.

QUESTIONS

Can the founder tell I used it?

No. Your reply is a normal email from you; the subject line is exactly what they wrote. Nothing is sent to them by Measure.

What if the pitch has no numbers?

THEIR NUMBERS says "Not stated." That is a fact about the email, not a judgment about the company — and it's often the most useful thing to know.

The numbers are in an attached deck, not the email.

The deck comes back to you unopened with an ATTACHED line naming it. Measure never opens attachments — reading decks would mean parsing your confidential files, and I'd rather be honest about the limit.

Does it work from Outlook, Apple Mail, my phone?

Yes — anything that can forward an email. It's an address, not an app.

Why an email address instead of an app or a Chrome extension?

Because an address needs no permission to your inbox. Every "connect your Gmail" tool has to read everything to summarize anything. Forwarding one email shares one email.

Why is the address at gmail.com?

It is a 2-step-verified Google mailbox run by a script on Google's own servers — nothing outside Google touches the mail. A custom domain is coming; the mechanics won't change.

What do you get out of this?

Proof that it saves people time. Measure is free while I learn what investors actually need. If it ever charges, existing users will hear from me first and nothing will change without notice.

Why can't I just enter my email address and get summaries?

Because that would mean giving Measure a login to your inbox — the one thing it is built never to ask for. Every "connect your Gmail" tool must read everything to summarize anything. A filter in your own settings gives the same zero-effort result while you keep the keys.

If my filter forwards a non-pitch by mistake?

Measure checks every message for pitch language before it does anything. Non-pitches are skipped unread — nothing is sent, and the copy is deleted within seven days. Keep the filter words narrow anyway.

Why offer to set it up on a call — isn't that old-fashioned?

Because it works. Superhuman onboarded every early user by hand and more than doubled the share who actually switched. Fifteen minutes with me and it's running; you'll also have my direct line if anything ever looks wrong.

Group inboxes and forwarding rules?

Yes — a shared deals@ address can forward, and Gmail filters can auto-forward pitches. The summary returns to whichever address forwarded.
