MEASURE

Privacy Policy

Effective 17 August 2026 · Applies to the Measure email relay and the Measure for Gmail add-on · Operator: Adil Mannan, Toronto, Canada · adil.mannan1@gmail.com
In one paragraph. Measure reads a startup pitch and shows its facts — what the company is, its numbers, its ask — in the founder's own words. It processes only the message you forward to it (relay) or the message you have open (add-on). It stores nothing about you, sends nothing to third parties, uses no artificial-intelligence vendor, sells no data, and shows no advertising. Everything runs on Google's own servers using fixed, deterministic rules.

1 · WHAT MEASURE IS

Measure exists in two forms. The email relay: you forward a pitch email to pitch.intake@gmail.com and it is returned to the address that forwarded it, with a factual summary on top. The Gmail add-on ("Measure for Gmail"): when you open an email in Gmail, a card beside it shows the same factual summary. Both are run by Adil Mannan as a sole operator in Toronto, Canada.

2 · WHAT INFORMATION IS PROCESSED

  • Add-on: only the email message you currently have open in Gmail, and the other messages in that same thread — accessed through Google's gmail.addons.current.message.readonly permission, which grants temporary access to the open message only while the add-on is displayed. Measure cannot see, search, or read any other part of your mailbox.
  • Relay: only the email you choose to forward to the intake address, and the address you forwarded it from (so the result can be returned to you).
  • Attachment names may be shown; attachment contents are never opened, scanned, or read.

3 · HOW IT IS PROCESSED

By a fixed set of text rules (regular expressions and word lists) running inside Google Apps Script on Google's infrastructure. No machine-learning model, no AI service, and no third-party API is used or contacted. The processing is deterministic: the same email always yields the same summary. Measure never scores, ranks, or recommends.

4 · WHAT IS STORED, AND FOR HOW LONG

  • Add-on: nothing. The card is generated when you open a message and discarded when you close it. Measure keeps no copy of your messages, no log of their content, and no record of which messages you opened.
  • Relay: the forwarded email remains in the intake mailbox for up to seven days, then is deleted and the mailbox's trash is emptied by an automatic job. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
  • Usage counting (relay only): a one-way, salted hash of the forwarding address is kept so the operator can count how many people use Measure. The hash cannot be reversed to an address and cannot be used to check whether any particular address used Measure.

5 · WHO CAN SEE YOUR DATA

No one reads your messages. Only the operator has administrative access to the systems, protected by two-step verification. Measure does not share, sell, rent, or transfer your information to any third party, and does not use it for advertising or profiling.

6 · WHAT MEASURE SENDS

  • Add-on: nothing is sent unless you tap a reply button, which opens a draft reply in your own Gmail with a suggested question. You edit it, and only you can send it. Measure never sends email on your behalf.
  • Look It Up buttons (add-on): each opens a public search page (Google, the sender's own website, LinkedIn) in your browser, with the company name or sender domain from the email as the query. Measure itself fetches nothing and receives nothing back; if you don't tap, nothing is opened.
  • Relay: the summary is returned only to the authenticated address that forwarded the pitch. Nothing is sent to the founder or to anyone else.

7 · GOOGLE API SERVICES USER DATA POLICY

Measure's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular: Google user data is used only to provide the summary feature you see; it is not used for advertising; it is not sold; it is not transferred to third parties except as necessary to provide the feature, comply with law, or with your explicit consent; and humans do not read it except with your consent, for security, or to comply with law.

8 · YOUR CHOICES

  • Add-on: uninstall it at any time from Gmail (Settings → Add-ons) or from your Google Account's third-party access page. Access ends immediately.
  • Relay: stop forwarding. To have anything deleted sooner than seven days, email adil.mannan1@gmail.com.

9 · CHILDREN

Measure is a business tool for investors and is not directed to children under 16.
